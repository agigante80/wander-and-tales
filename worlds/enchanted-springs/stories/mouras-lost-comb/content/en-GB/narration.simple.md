# The Moura's Lost Comb

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

This morning the village square is full of worried voices. The Old Fountain has
gone quiet. Its water sits still and grey, and nobody can fill their jugs.

You stand at the edge of the square. An old woman says, "The fountain is fed by
the Enchanted Spring, up in the hills. Something has gone wrong up there. Will you
go and see?"

You pick up your bag. You look at each other and nod. The path winds up into the
green hills.

*What is your hero name? Which magics do you choose: Bloom, Truth, Starlight, or
Riversong?*

## Stop 1: The Quiet Spring

You climb until you reach a clear spring among mossy stones. Beside it sits a
gentle maiden with long dark hair. This is the Moura Encantada. She looks sad and
a little shy.

"My golden comb is gone," she says softly. "Without it I cannot wake the water,
and the fountain in your village stays quiet. But I am too shy to go looking."

She will only talk to a calm, kind voice.

*How do you put her at ease? Could you speak gently to the spring with Riversong?
What kind thing could you say or do?*

When the Moura trusts you, she smiles. "A shiny trail leads off into the ferns,"
she says. "Follow it, and please be gentle with whoever you find."

## Stop 2: The Stepping Stones

The trail leads to a shallow stream. To cross it you must step on the right
stones. Each stone has a small carved mark: a leaf, a star, a wave, then a leaf, a
star, and a gap where the next mark should be.

A little voice in the water hums, "Leaf, star, wave, leaf, star, and then?"

*What mark comes next in the pattern? Which stone do you step on?*

When you step on the wave stone, the water steadies and lets you across. On the
far bank, the shiny trail leads under a little stone bridge.

## Stop 3: The Trasgo's Nook

Under the bridge is a cosy nook full of bright, shiny things. In the middle sits a
small goblin in a red cap, the Trasgo, playing happily with the golden comb. He
grins at you.

"I found it. It is shiny. It is mine now," he says, holding it tight. He is not
mean. He just loves shiny things and a good game, and he is a little lonely down
here.

*How do you make friends with the Trasgo? Could you offer a swap, a riddle, or a
game? What would a playful goblin like?*

When you trade something he loves, or win his game, the Trasgo laughs and hands
over the comb. "Come and visit me again," he says. "It is quiet under here."

## Stop 4: The Comb Returned

You carry the golden comb back up to the spring. The Moura Encantada is waiting.
Very carefully, you place the comb in her hands.

She draws it once through her long hair. The water lights up clear and bright and
lifts in a gentle, glowing arc.

*How do you give the comb back kindly? What do you say to the Moura?*

"Thank you," she says. "You were gentle, and you made a friend instead of an
enemy. The water will sing again."

## The Spring Sings Again

You hurry back down to the village. The Old Fountain is bubbling clear and bright,
and the water almost sings. Children laugh and fill their jugs. The Moura sends a
cool, sweet blessing down with the water.

You did it together. You were kind, and you were full of ideas. Well done.
