# The Moura's Lost Comb: Rules for the Grown-Up

The Guide for the Grown-Up explains how the game works (the difficulty bands, the
energy stars, helping, twists, all of it). This page has only what is special
about this adventure. A failed roll is never a defeat; nobody loses.

## Setting up

- Print the map, or sketch it, or lay it out with household objects: a Start, four
  stops, and a Goal on a winding path. Each player puts a small figure (a
  building-brick minifigure works well) on the Start.
- Each player chooses three magics from the four in this world: Bloom, Truth,
  Starlight, and Riversong. A mix across the players works best, because combining
  magics is half the fun.
- Give each player five energy stars (five small tokens or bricks). These are
  spent and refilled during play.
- Build the things that come up (the golden comb, the stepping stones, the
  Trasgo's shiny hoard) from household bits and bricks as you go. Anything can be
  anything.
- Keep your dice and the dice-band table from the back of the kit beside you.

## The surprise

Once at each stop the players may roll for a twist; read the matching line. The
Guide explains how it works.

| Roll | Surprise |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Galo de Barcelos crows nearby and points you to one free hint. |
| 2 | You find a glint of water-light: take back one energy star. |
| 3 | A kind breeze off the hills: the next challenge is Easy. |
| 4 | The Moura hums a few notes of her water-song. All is well; carry on. |
| 5 | A friendly creature lets you repeat one roll later, whenever you like. |
| 6 | Almond blossom drifts past and everyone smiles for one happy turn. |

## How it ends

The golden comb is not won in a battle. The Trasgo, who has it, is not a villain:
he is a playful, lonely goblin who only wanted a shiny treasure and someone to
play with. The adventure turns on befriending him, not beating him, and on being
gentle with the shy Moura Encantada. Let both of those land softly; that kindness
is the whole point. See the puzzles page for how to play each stop, and for the
solutions.
