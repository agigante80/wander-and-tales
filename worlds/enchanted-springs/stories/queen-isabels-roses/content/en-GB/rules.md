# Queen Isabel's Roses: Rules for the Grown-Up

The Guide for the Grown-Up explains how the game works (the difficulty bands, the
energy stars, helping, twists, all of it). This page has only what is special
about this adventure. A failed roll is never a defeat; nobody loses.

## Setting up

- Print the map, or sketch it, or lay it out with household objects: a Start, four
  stops, and a Goal on a winding path. Each player puts a small figure on the
  Start.
- Each player chooses three magics from the four in this world: Bloom, Truth,
  Starlight, and Riversong. Bloom shines in this story, but a mix works best.
- Give each player five energy stars (five small tokens or bricks). These are
  spent and refilled during play.
- Build the things that come up (the covered basket, the loaves, the roses) from
  household bits and bricks as you go. Anything can be anything.
- Keep your dice and the dice-band table from the back of the kit beside you.

## The surprise

Once at each stop the players may roll for a twist; read the matching line. The
Guide explains how it works.

| Roll | Surprise |
|---|---|
| 1 | A baker slips you a warm loaf and one free hint. |
| 2 | You find a coin in the cobbles: take back one energy star. |
| 3 | A kind neighbour points the way: the next challenge is Easy. |
| 4 | A waft of rose scent drifts by. All is well; carry on. |
| 5 | Queen Isabel's blessing lets you repeat one roll later, whenever you like. |
| 6 | A bell rings over the river and everyone feels brave for one turn. |

## How it ends

There is no villain in this story. The stern watchman is only doing his duty, and
he is won over by honesty, not defeated. The climax is the gentle miracle the
legend is famous for: the bread the children carry turns to roses, so a secret
kindness is protected and nobody is scolded. Play it as a reward for honesty and
quiet generosity, not a trick to fool anyone. The lesson is that kindness given
softly, asking nothing back, is the kind that blooms. See the puzzles page for how
to play each stop, and for the solutions.
