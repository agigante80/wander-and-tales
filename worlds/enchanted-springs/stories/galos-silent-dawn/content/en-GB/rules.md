# The Galo's Silent Dawn: Rules for the Grown-Up

The Guide for the Grown-Up explains how the game works (the difficulty bands, the
energy stars, helping, twists, all of it). This page has only what is special
about this adventure. A failed roll is never a defeat; nobody loses.

## Setting up

- Print the map, or sketch it, or lay it out with household objects: a Start, four
  stops, and a Goal on a winding path. Each player puts a small figure on the
  Start.
- Each player chooses one or two magics from the five in this world: Bloom, Truth,
  Starlight, Riversong, or Birdsong. A mix across the players works best.
- Give each player five energy stars (five small tokens or bricks). These are
  spent and refilled during play.
- Build the things that come up (the silver cup, the jackdaw's shiny nest) from
  household bits and bricks as you go. Anything can be anything.
- Keep your dice and the dice-band table from the back of the kit beside you.

## The surprise

Once at each stop the players may roll for a twist; read the matching line. The
Guide explains how it works.

| Roll | Surprise |
|---|---|
| 1 | A market seller offers you a warm pastel and one free hint. |
| 2 | You find a dropped coin that glints: take back one energy star. |
| 3 | A cool breeze off the river: the next challenge is Easy. |
| 4 | The Galo gives an encouraging cluck. All is well; carry on. |
| 5 | A passing pilgrim lets you repeat one roll later, whenever you like. |
| 6 | A church bell rings the hour and everyone feels brave for one turn. |

## How it ends

There is no thief in this story and no villain. The "crime" is an innocent mix-up:
a shy jackdaw who gathered the shiny cup for her nest, not knowing the trouble it
caused. The heart of the adventure is clearing a wrongly blamed stranger and being
gentle with the timid jackdaw, so the Galo de Barcelos can crow the truth at
dawn. Let the moment when the townsfolk realise their unfairness land softly; the
lesson is to be fair and kind, especially to a stranger. See the puzzles page for
how to play each stop, and for the solutions.
