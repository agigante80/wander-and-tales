# The Singing Spring: Idea Bank

*Improv fuel for when a child goes happily off-script. Pull from these freely; none
of it can break the story. Everything here is brave and kind, in keeping with these
hills, where heroes win by imagination, courage, and heart, never by force.*

## The three hero qualities

**Imagination:** dreaming up a new idea, asking the one good question, trying the
surprising thing, picturing a riddle's answer, reading the lay of the land, turning a
problem upside down, remembering an old story that helps.

**Courage:** taking the first step on a dark path, speaking gently to something
fearsome, owning up to a mistake, trying again after a miss, standing in front of a
friend, keeping a steady voice when your knees shake.

**Heart:** sharing food, asking a stranger's name, offering friendship, comforting
a sad creature, keeping a promise, cheering up a companion, choosing the kind way
over the easy way.

## Helpful friends along the way

- **Bramble the Satyr** pops up with jokes and hints whenever the heroes are stuck.
- **Chiron the Centaur**, gentlest of teachers, might trot by to offer a calm word.
- The **Singing Nymphs** of the spring reward kindness with a song.
- The **Riddle Sphinx** is stern at her gate but warm once you have answered.
- Even the **Lonely Cyclops** becomes a gentle friend who carries the heroes on his
  shoulders for fun.

## Handy things to find or build

- The **golden thread** for any maze or dark place.
- The **honey cake**, and other small gifts: a ripe fig, a flower crown, a smooth
  river stone, a token of friendship.
- The **little lyre**, a set of pan-pipes, a bright song, a brave shout that echoes.
- An olive branch for peace, a water skin, a sun hat, a map drawn in the dust, a
  lucky knucklebone.

## Places to wander

A sunlit olive grove, a marble temple full of echoes, a cool cave behind a
waterfall, a meadow of poppies, a harbour of little fishing boats, a hilltop where
you can see the whole sea.

## Mini-challenges

Cross stepping stones in the right order, match a god to their symbol, calm a
nervous goat, finish a half-remembered song, share out fruit so everyone gets a
fair piece, find the one path that catches the morning sun.

## Happy rewards

A laurel crown, a ride on the cyclops's shoulders, a new riddle to take home, a
song the nymphs teach you, a basket of figs, or a wink from a goddess. Every reward
is something to feel proud of; nothing is ever taken away.
