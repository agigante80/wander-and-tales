# The Honey-Cake Feast

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are no
wrong answers here, only kind new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

Today the Olive Town is having a feast! For the very first time, the big gentle
Cyclops is coming down from the hills to join everyone, because he is a friend now and
no longer lonely. To welcome him, the town is baking the largest honey cake anyone has
ever seen.

But the honey jar is empty. Old Daphne, who keeps the olive grove, calls you to her.
"You two have busy hands and kind hearts," she says. "Will you help gather what we
need, and bring our big friend down to the table?"

She gives you an empty jar and a warm smile. "Up here we do not feast by leaving
anyone out. We share, and we always make room for one more."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: The Hill of Bees

Up in a sunny meadow live the Hill Bees, who keep the sweetest honey in all the
hills. They buzz in busy circles around their hive. If you rush or grab, they will zip
away.

*How could you ask the bees to share? Could you move slowly and stay calm? Could you
offer them some flowers first? What do you try?*

When you are gentle and ask kindly, the Hill Bees hum happily and fill your jar to the
brim with golden honey. "Thank you, friends," they buzz.

## Stop 2: Milo's Kitchen

Back in town, Milo the baker has made an enormous honey cake. But how do you share it
so that everyone gets a piece, even the huge Cyclops, who needs a very big slice?

*Let us count together. How many friends are coming? How can you cut the cake so
nobody is left out, and the giant gets enough too? A fair piece is not always the same
size.*

When you count the guests and share the cake fairly, every plate is filled, with a
great big slice saved for your giant friend.

## Stop 3: Fetching the Friend

You climb up to the Cyclops's cave. He is happy to see you, but shy. "A whole town?"
he says, looking down at his big feet. "What if I am too big? What if they are scared
of me?"

*He needs a little courage. What kind thing could you say? Could you tell him he is
welcome? Could you show him the slice you saved, and walk down together? What do you
do?*

When you cheer him on with kind words, the giant smiles all the way across his big
face. He takes your hand, and down the hill you go together.

## Everyone at the Table

As the sun goes down, the whole Olive Town sits at one long table under the olive
trees. And right in the middle, with the biggest slice of all, sits the Cyclops,
laughing with his new friends.

Nobody is left out. Every plate is full, every face is happy. High on the mountain,
kind Athena smiles down at the feast.

You did it together. You were brave, you were full of ideas, and most of all, you were kind.
Well done, heroes.
