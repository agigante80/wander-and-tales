# Athena's Little Owl: Puzzles and Solutions

*For your eyes, not the children's. Each stop lists its band, what the players need to
work out, the intended solution, and a gentle fallback so a stuck child always moves
forward. More than one good idea should work; reward fresh ideas and kindness over the
"right" answer.*

## Stop 1: The Foggy Square (Easy)

**The challenge.** Glaux the owl is too frightened to be carried. The players must
help her feel safe enough to trust them and hop onto a waiting hand.

**Intended solution.** This is a Heart and Observation moment. The kind move is to
slow right down: lower their voices, move gently, hold still, and let the owl come to
them rather than grabbing for her. Holding out a steady hand and waiting works
beautifully.

**Fallback.** If the children rush and Glaux hops away, have Daphne hum a soft tune,
or let a player spend an energy star to sit quietly until the owl creeps closer. Any
gentle idea wins her trust and tops up an energy star.

**Make it simpler.** Ask the littlest one to whisper "hello, little owl" and hold out
one open hand, and let Glaux hop straight on.

## Stop 2: The Whispering Paths (Normal)

**The challenge.** Three paths climb into the fog and all look the same. The players
must find the one that leads to Athena's temple.

**Intended solution.** This is an Observation and Logic moment. The good idea is to
look down and follow the trail of soft grey feathers Glaux shed as she fell. Asking
**Bramble the Satyr** for a hint, or letting the owl herself turn her head toward
home, works just as well.

**Fallback.** If the children are unsure, have Bramble pop up and point the way with a
joke, or let them spend an energy star to spot the feathers in the mist. Getting
through with help still counts as a full success and tops up an energy star.

**Make it simpler.** Lay out a little line of feathers (or torn paper scraps) and let
the child simply follow them with a finger to the right path.

## Stop 3: The High Perch (Normal)

**The challenge.** Glaux is tired and afraid to fly the last stretch up to her high
marble perch through the mist. The players must help her up.

**Intended solution.** This is a Heart and Spatial moment. Any kind, gentle idea
works: lifting a steady branch to bridge the gap, making a careful step for her small
feet, or simply cheering her on until she finds the courage to flutter up. There is no
wrong way to be kind here.

**Fallback.** If the children freeze, have Glaux give a small, hopeful hoot and lean
toward them, and let a single encouraging word be enough. The moment they offer help
of any kind, the little owl reaches her perch.

**Make it simpler.** Let the child lift Glaux gently in two cupped hands and place her
on her perch, cheering "up you go" as they do.

## The ending

There is no battle and no loser. When Glaux is safely home, kind **Athena** opens her
calm grey eyes, the morning fog lifts, and the sun pours warm and golden over the
**Olive Town**. The heroes have won the only way anyone wins here, by being brave,
kind, and full of ideas.
