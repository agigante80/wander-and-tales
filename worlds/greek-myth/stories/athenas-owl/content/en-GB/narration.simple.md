# Athena's Little Owl

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only kind new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

One grey, misty morning a tiny owl came tumbling out of the fog and landed, all
ruffled, in the middle of the Olive Town. Her name is Glaux, and she belongs to
kind Athena, high on the hill of white marble. But the fog is thick, and poor
Glaux cannot find her way home.

Old Daphne, who keeps the olive grove, calls you to her. "You two have sharp eyes
and kind hearts," she says. "Will you help little Glaux find her way back up to
Athena?"

She tucks a soft scarf around the owl and smiles. "Up here we do not help by being
the biggest or the fastest. We help by being brave, kind, and full of ideas."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: The Foggy Square

Glaux sits in the middle of the square, very small and very scared. Her feathers
are puffed up. Her big round eyes blink fast. When you step too quickly, she hops
away.

*How could you help her feel safe? Could you move slowly and speak softly? Could
you hold out a steady hand for her to perch on? What do you try?*

When you are calm and gentle, Glaux stops trembling. She hops onto your hand and
gives a happy little hoot. Now she trusts you.

## Stop 2: The Whispering Paths

At the edge of town, three little paths climb up into the mist. They all look the
same. Only one leads to Athena's temple.

A goat-legged satyr peeks over a wall. It is Bramble, who loves a traveller and a
good joke. He hums and tips his head, ready to help if you ask nicely.

*How will you choose the right path? Look closely. Glaux dropped soft grey feathers
as she fell. Could you follow them back up? Could you ask Bramble for a hint?*

When you follow the trail of feathers, or ask kind Bramble, you find the right path
and climb up through the mist together.

## Stop 3: The High Perch

At the top stands a little marble temple. High up is Glaux's cosy perch, but she is
tired and the last of the fog still hides it. She is afraid to fly up alone.

*This is not about being strong. How could you help her up? Could you lift a steady
branch like a bridge? Could you cheer her on so she feels brave? What do you do?*

When you help her with kindness, Glaux flutters up to her perch and snuggles down,
safe and warm at last.

## Glaux Comes Home

As the little owl settles, two calm grey eyes open in the temple. It is Athena,
goddess of good ideas, and she is smiling at you.

The morning fog melts away. Sunlight pours over the Olive Town below, warm and
golden. Glaux hoots a thank-you from her perch.

You did it together. You were brave, you were full of ideas, and most of all, you were
kind. Well done, heroes.
