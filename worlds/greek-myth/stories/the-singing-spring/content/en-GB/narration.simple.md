# The Singing Spring

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

The Singing Spring has gone quiet. High in the hills, the water used to sing all
day, and its song kept every well in the town full and cool. Now the song has
stopped. The wells are low. The olive trees are thirsty.

Old Daphne, who keeps the olive grove, calls you to her. "You two have bright
ideas and kind hearts," she says. "Will you climb the hill and wake the spring?"

She gives you three gifts: a ball of golden thread, a honey cake, and a little
lyre. Then she smiles. "Up here we do not win by being the strongest. We win by
being brave, kind, and full of ideas."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: The Riddle Gate

You climb the sunny road and come to a tall marble gate. On the steps sits a
sphinx, big as a lion, with golden wings and a kind, gentle face.

"None may pass," she says, "until they answer my riddle." She is not cross. She
likes a good thinking face.

*What could you do? Could you listen closely and think it through together? A brave
guess is allowed too. Try something and see.*

When you answer, the sphinx dips her wings and lets you by. "Well thought," she
purrs. The gate swings open.

## Stop 2: The Winding Maze

Past the gate is a maze of old stone walls. Every path looks the same. It would be
easy to get lost.

A cheerful satyr with goat legs pops his head over a wall. It is Bramble. He hums a
little tune and points down one path with a wink.

*How will you keep from getting lost? You have a ball of golden thread. What could
you do with it? Could you ask Bramble for a hint?*

When you tie the thread at the start and follow it on, you never lose your way. You
come out the other side, into the evening light.

## Stop 3: The Lonely Giant

At last you reach the spring. But a huge giant sits in front of it, with one big
eye and a deep, sad face. He folds his arms. He looks fearsome.

But listen. He sniffs. Is he, maybe, a little bit lonely? Nobody ever comes to
visit him.

*This is not a fight. What kind thing could you do? You have a honey cake to share.
You could ask his name. You could tell him he is not alone. What do you try?*

When you are kind to him, the giant's big eye fills with happy tears. He shuffles
aside. "Thank you for being my friends," he says softly.

## The Spring Sings Again

Now you stand at the quiet spring. You take out the little lyre and play a simple
tune, straight from the heart.

The water shivers. Gentle nymphs rise up in the spray, laughing and singing. The
spring bursts back to life! Far below, every well in the town fills up cool and
clear.

High on the mountain, kind Athena smiles down at you. You did it together. You were
brave, you were full of ideas, and most of all, you were kind. Well done, heroes.
