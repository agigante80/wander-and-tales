# Idea Bank: The Blossom Mountains of Yamato

*Improv prompts for the grown-up. When the children want to try something the page
did not plan for, reach in here. Nothing is a wrong answer; every idea bends toward
calm, cleverness, and nobody losing.*

## If the heroes are stuck

- Kon the Fox trots out of the bamboo, asks a tiny riddle whose answer is the next
  clue, then winks and slips away.
- A breeze carries a single cherry petal that drifts to land on the right path.
- Granny Yuki's voice floats up the valley with the first line of an old tale that
  holds a hint.
- Let the heroes spend an energy star to "remember a saying Grandmother taught them".

## Friendly faces to drop in

- A patient crane standing in the rice field who will share the way for a kind bow.
- A round, jolly tanuki who knows a shortcut but keeps getting distracted.
- A tiny tea-kettle spirit who has lost its lid and could use a hand.
- A sleepy cat curled on a warm step who only wants someone to sit a while.

## Gentle troubles to solve

- A bridge that will only hold those who cross it slowly and gently.
- A row of paper lanterns to light in the right order so the path glows.
- A muddle of festival stalls to sort back into place.
- A line of stepping stones across the river to read and cross in turn.

## Ways to win that are not winning

- Make the stone lion-dogs smile instead of out-arguing them.
- Trade a rice cake for a story instead of for the way through.
- Ring the little bell softly until a sleeping friend stirs, rather than shouting.
- Leave the place calmer and kinder than you found it.

## Yamato flavour to sprinkle

- Cherry blossom on the breeze, the click of bamboo, the smell of warm rice.
- Paper lanterns glowing along a path, a red torii gate, a silver moon on the river.
- The hush of a misty mountain, the soft ring of a temple bell, a folded paper crane.
