# The Firefly Lanterns

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

Tonight is the summer Lantern Festival in the Lantern Village. Every year the paper
lanterns glow, and the whole village dances under their warm light. But something is
wrong. The lanterns will not light. They hang grey and dark in the evening air.

The reason is a quiet one. Inside each lantern lives a shy little Firefly Spirit, and
tonight every one of them has flown off to hide. They are deep in the Whispering
Bamboo Grove, in the dark, too timid to come out.

Granny Yuki the Tale-Keeper calls you to her side. "You two have heads full of ideas and
kind hearts," she says. "Will you go to the grove and gently coax the firefly spirits back,
so our festival can glow?"

She gives you three gifts: a paper lantern, a ball of red thread, and a soft, sweet
rice cake. Then she smiles. "Up in the hills we do not win by being loud or strong. We
win by being brave, kind, and full of ideas."

*What is your hero name? Will you carry Imagination, Courage, or Kindness?*

## Stop 1: The Dark Bamboo

You climb the path to the Whispering Bamboo Grove. The tall stalks click and whisper
in the wind. It is dark in here, and very still. Somewhere close, tiny lights blink on
and off, then hide again. The firefly spirits are here, but they are shy.

A russet fox steps softly from the green shadows. It is Kon the Fox, with bright,
lively eyes and a little leaf on his head. He gives you a friendly wink.

*The spirits are timid. What kind thing could you do? Could you move gently and speak
softly? You have a sweet rice cake to share. Could you sit very still and let them come
to you?*

When you are calm and kind, one little light drifts near. Then another. Soon a cloud of
tiny golden fireflies floats around you, no longer afraid. "Well done," whispers Kon.
"They trust you now."

## Stop 2: The Lantern Path

The fireflies will follow you home, but only if they feel safe. A long path of empty
paper lanterns leads down the hill to the village. If you light them in the right
order, a gentle path of light will guide the shy spirits all the way.

But which lantern first? They come in many sizes, and your ball of red thread runs from
one to the next, tying them in a line.

*How will you light them so the spirits feel safe? Could you go from the smallest
lantern to the biggest? Could you simply follow the red thread from lantern to lantern?
Could you ask Kon the Fox for a hint?*

When you find the gentle order and light them one by one, a soft road of golden light
opens up. The little fireflies bob along it happily, following you down the hill.

## Stop 3: The Great Lantern

At the top of the festival square stands the Great Lantern, the biggest of them all. To
light it, the shyest little firefly of all must fly up inside. But this one is the most
timid, and it hangs back, trembling, afraid of the great dark lantern.

This is not a fight. The little firefly just needs to feel brave.

*What could you say to help it? Could you speak in a soft, calm voice? Could you tell it
that you believe in it, and that you will be right here? Could you show it how brave it
already is?*

When you cheer it on with calm and kindness, the little firefly takes a deep breath and
flies up. The Great Lantern fills with warm light, the brightest of all.

## The Festival Glows

One by one, every lantern in the village glows golden. The dark is gone. The firefly
spirits swirl and dance in the warm air, looping in happy circles, no longer shy at all.

The whole village cheers and laughs and begins to dance. Drums beat softly. The lanterns
sway like a sky full of small warm moons.

When morning comes, warm Amaterasu the Sun-Kami lifts over the hills and smiles down at
you. You did it together. You were brave, you were kind, and most of all, you were full of ideas.
Well done, heroes.
