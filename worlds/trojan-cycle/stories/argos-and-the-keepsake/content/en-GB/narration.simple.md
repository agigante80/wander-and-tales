# Argos and the Lost Keepsake

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

The ships are nearly ready to sail home across the sea. There is happy bustle all
along the Ship Camp, with sails being mended and figs being packed. But near one
tent sits your friend Lykos, and he is crying.

"I have lost my keepsake," he says. "A little token, carved from olive wood, that
my grandmother gave me. I carried it all this long way, and now it is gone,
somewhere out on the windswept shore. We sail at sunset. I cannot leave it behind."

Odysseus the Bright kneels beside you. "A friend is sad, and a treasure is lost,"
he says. "That is a job for bright ideas and faithful hearts. Will you go and find
it before the ships sail?"

He gives you three gifts: the Conch Horn, the Basket of Honey and Figs, and
Penelope's Thread. Then he whistles, and up trots a loyal old dog with a grey muzzle
and a wagging tail.

"This is Argos the Faithful Hound," says Odysseus. "He never forgets a friend or a
scent. Follow his nose, follow each other, and leave no one behind."

*What is your hero name? Will you be a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of
Loyalty?*

## Stop 1: Following Argos

Argos puts his nose to the sand and sets off along the beach. He stops at a little
mark in the sand, then at another, then he looks back at you and waits.

There are tracks here, and small clues too: a dropped fig, a footprint, a scuff
where someone sat down to rest.

*What could you do? Could you look closely at the tracks with Argos and read which
way your friend walked? Which clues point one way, and which point the other?*

When you follow the right trail, Argos gives a happy little bark and trots on. You
are on the scent now.

## Stop 2: The Tide Pools

The trail leads you in among the sunlit rock pools, where the sea has left little
ponds full of shells and crabs. The keepsake brushed past one of these pools, but
which one?

There are three pools. Beside the first lies a white shell. Beside the second lies
a striped shell. Beside the third lies a smooth grey pebble.

Argos sniffs and tells you two things. The scent went past a striped shell, not a
white one. And the scent did not stop where the pebble was.

*Can you remember the clues? Which pool had the striped shell and no pebble? That is
where your friend rested, and dropped a thread from his cloak.*

When you work out the second pool, you find the thread caught on a rock, pointing
the way on. Argos wags his tail. Well thought!

## Stop 3: The Far Dune

The trail ends at a lonely dune, far along the shore, where the wind has been
piling the sand all day. Somewhere here the keepsake fell, and the wind has half
buried it.

Argos sniffs in big circles, then stops at one spot and looks at you, ears up.

*What could you do? Could you watch where Argos points his nose and dig there, very
gently, together? Look for a corner of olive wood in the sand.*

When you brush the sand away, there it is: the little carved token, warm from the
sun. Argos digs the last of it free with one careful paw and drops it into your
hands.

## Nobody Left Behind

You hurry back along the shore to the Ship Camp, the keepsake held safe. When Lykos
sees it, his face lights up like the sunrise.

"You found it! You came back for it!" he cries, and he hugs the little token, and
then he hugs Argos, who wags so hard he nearly falls over.

Odysseus the Bright smiles and says, "You did not leave a friend behind. That is the
best idea there ever was." Out on the bright waves, Thetis the Sea-Mother
lifts her gentle face and smiles upon you.

The ships can sail home now, every friend and every treasure safe aboard. Well done,
heroes. Well done, Argos. Nobody was left behind.
