# Idea Bank: The Windswept Shores of Troy

*Improv prompts for the grown-up. When the children want to try something the page
did not plan for, reach in here. Nothing is a wrong answer; every idea bends toward
imagination, friendship, and nobody losing.*

## If the heroes are stuck

- Odysseus the Bright wanders past, rubs his beard, and asks a question that is
  really a hint in disguise.
- Argos the Faithful Hound trots up, sniffs, and points his nose the right way.
- A gull wheels overhead and drops a feather on the thing that matters.
- Let the heroes spend an energy star to "remember a trick an old sailor taught them".

## Friendly faces to drop in

- A weary city guard who would rather share a fig than keep a gate shut.
- A dolphin who will show the safe channel for a kind word.
- A child on the walls who waves, curious, and could become a friend.
- An old shipwright who knows just how to make the timbers balance.

## Gentle troubles to solve

- A heavy thing to move that only works if everyone lifts together.
- A row of ships to count, or planks to measure with Penelope's thread.
- A sad song across the water that asks to be answered, not fled.
- A gate that opens not to a push but to a gift and a friendly word.

## Ways to win that are not winning

- End the quarrel with a surprise of friendship instead of a show of strength.
- Trade a basket of figs for a story rather than for the way through.
- Sing back to the lonely sirens until their song turns glad.
- Leave every shore kinder and every friend closer than you found them.

## Aegean flavour to sprinkle

- Salt wind and the creak of timber, painted eyes on the prows, white sails snapping.
- Honey-coloured walls in the sun, olive trees, the smell of figs and cook-fires.
- The wine-dark sea at dawn, a faithful dog on the sand, a conch horn far off.
