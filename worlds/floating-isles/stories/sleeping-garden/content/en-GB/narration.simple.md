# The Sleeping Garden

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

This morning you wake up and the whole island is quiet. Too quiet. You look out
of the window of the House of the Little Mages. The Great Garden is grey and
still, as if it has fallen asleep. The flowers are shut. The fountain makes no
sound.

You are two little mages. You pick up your bag. You look at each other. "Let us
go and wake it up," you say together. The golden path is waiting.

*What is your mage name? Which magic do you choose: Light, Grow, Change, or
Voice?*

## Stop 1: The Vine Gate

You come to a tall gate. Green vines have tied it shut with a big knot of leaves.
The gate will not open.

A soft grey cat steps out of the mist. It is the Mist Cat. It purrs and looks at
the knot, then at you. It seems to want to help.

*How do you open the way? Could a magic make a gap? Could you ask the vines
kindly to move? Try something and see.*

When the gate swings open, the first sleepy flower opens one petal. The garden is
waking, just a little.

## Stop 2: The Flower Bed

Now you reach a bed of flowers. Four of them have drooped and lost their order. A
tiny voice hums a little rhyme: "First the sun, then the sea, then the berry,
then fly free."

The flowers want to stand in the right colours, but they have forgotten how.

*Which colour is the sun? Which is the sea? Can you set the four flowers in the
order the rhyme sings?*

When the flowers stand in their order, they open wide and turn their faces up.
Two more corners of the garden turn green again.

## Stop 3: The Talking Fountain

You come to a stone fountain. It is not singing. It is not even dripping. The
water sits still and grey, and the fountain looks a little sad. You lean close.
"Hello?" you whisper. The fountain sighs. It wants to feel happy again.

*What could you do to cheer it up? Maybe a song. Maybe a joke. Maybe a kind word.
What do you try?*

When the fountain feels happy, the water leaps up and sparkles. It begins to sing
again. Now only the very heart of the garden is still asleep.

## The Heart of the Garden

At the centre of the garden you find a small, sad sprite curled up tight. It was
the sprite who sent the garden to sleep. Not to be unkind. It was only lonely,
and it did not know how to ask for a friend.

There is no fight here. There never was a real villain.

*What do you say to the sprite? How can you show it that it is not alone?*

When you offer the sprite your friendship, it smiles for the first time in a long
while. All around you, the Great Garden wakes up at once: flowers open, the
fountain sings, and the island is bright and warm again.

You did it together. You were kind, and you were full of ideas. Well done.
