# The Sleeping Garden: Idea Bank

*Improv fuel for when a child goes happily off-script. Pull from these freely;
none of it can break the story. Everything here is gentle and helpful, in keeping
with the Isles, where magic never harms.*

## Powers by magic

**Light:** a firefly eye that lights up clues, a sun-gaze that reads hidden
messages, a kind flash that calms a nervous creature, a glowing compass, a curtain
of light to hide behind, a guiding beacon, a rainbow bridge.

**Grow:** a gardener's hand that plants a bridge, a leaping ivy vine, a shield of
leaves so nobody is hurt, a speedy seed, a moss trampoline, an umbrella flower for
shelter, friendly roots that lend a hand.

**Change:** a transforming touch (a stone into a key), a mini-and-maxi spell that
shrinks or grows things, chameleon colours, a water trick (ice into mist), a
wildcard key, a path of stepping stones, a mirror echo.

**Voice:** animal chat, a whisper to objects (asking what they need), a calming
song, a finding echo, a friendly request, a joke-teller that opens grumpy gates, a
call for friends.

## Items by magic

- **Light:** a lantern of stars, a rainbow magnifying glass, night glasses, a
  candle that never goes out.
- **Grow:** a jumping seed, an endless watering can, giant gloves, a pocket
  flowerpot.
- **Change:** a chalk wand, a colour-changing glove, a transforming die, a magic
  ice cube.
- **Voice:** a talking conch, an animal whistle, a leaf megaphone, a notebook that
  listens.

## Items for anyone

A cloak of courage, a compass of friendship, a jar of glimmer, a map that draws
itself, a toy master key, a little bell of hints, a pocket mirror, a notebook of
secrets, a bottomless bag, a lucky stone. Each item is used once per adventure and
recharges by helping or by solving a puzzle.

## Friendly creatures

The **Mist Cat** (gives hints), the **Librarian Owl** (loves riddles), the
**Bridge Tortoise** (lets you cross on her shell), **Messenger Butterflies** (carry
notes and free hints), the **Talking Flowers** (chat in soft voices), and the
**Lonely Sprite** once it has a friend. None of them wishes any harm.

## Magical places

An echo cave, a mushroom bridge, a mirror lake, a cloud swing, a library of
leaves, a clearing full of fireflies.

## Mini-challenges

Follow a trail of petals, find the matching pair of stones, sort sounds in order,
answer a guardian's riddle, find the way through a maze of flowers, remember a
pattern of stars.

## Happy rewards

A star sticker, a little dance, a hug and a thank you, a free hint, a new seed to
plant, or all your energy stars back at once. Every reward is something to feel
glad about; nothing is ever taken away.
