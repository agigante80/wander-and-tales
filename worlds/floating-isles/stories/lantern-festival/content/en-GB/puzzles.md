# The Lost Lantern Festival: Puzzles and Solutions

*For your eyes, not the children's. Each stop lists its band, what the players need
to work out, the intended solution, and a gentle fallback so a stuck child always
moves forward. More than one good idea should work; reward good ideas and kindness
over the "right" answer.*

## Stop 1: The Dim Square (Easy)

**The challenge.** The last sky lantern has gone cold and dark, and the players need
to relight it and work out where the other lanterns have drifted off to.

**Intended solutions.** Any of the magics can help:
- **Light** wakes the little flame at once, and lights up the faint trail leading to
  the clouds.
- **Change** turns a cold ember or a pebble into a warm, glowing one.
- **Voice**: ask the lantern itself, or the Mist Cat, where the others went; both are
  happy to point up toward the cloud bank.

**Fallback.** If a roll misses, the Mist Cat nudges the lantern and looks pointedly
up at the trail of light, then the players spend an energy star or combine two magics
to finish. Nobody waits in the dark for long.

## Stop 2: The Cloud Bank (Normal)

**The challenge.** Several lanterns are hidden inside a bank of soft clouds. The
players must work out which clouds hold a lantern, and how to reach them.

**Intended solution.** The clue is that a hidden lantern makes its cloud glow softly
and feel warm, while the empty clouds stay cool and grey. This is an **observation**
moment: pick out the warm, glowing clouds. Then reach them with **Grow** (a vine
ladder or a tall reed), with **Voice** (ask the Messenger Butterfly or the breeze to
carry them down), or with a combined magic.

**Fallback.** If the children are unsure, have the Messenger Butterfly hover beside
one glowing cloud to show them what "warm and glowing" looks like, then let them find
the rest. Getting there with a nudge still counts as a full success, and tops up an
energy star.

## Stop 3: The High Ledge (Normal)

**The challenge.** The biggest festival lantern has caught on a high rocky ledge,
out of reach. The players must bring it down gently, with no one climbing into any
danger.

**Intended solutions.** This is a kindness-and-care puzzle, not a daring climb:
- **Grow** a thick cushion of leaves or a soft vine below to catch the lantern.
- **Voice**: ask the evening breeze for the gentlest nudge to float it free.
- **Change** a pebble into a soft hook on a long grown stem to lift it down.
Any plan that brings it down safely works. Make a point of the fact that nobody needs
to climb or risk a fall.

**Fallback.** If the children freeze, have the breeze give a small, hopeful puff that
rocks the lantern, so they see it could be coaxed rather than grabbed. The moment they
try a gentle idea, let the lantern drift down into their hands.

## Nimbo the Cloud-Whale Calf (no roll)

This is the resolution, not a puzzle, so there is no band and no roll. Up in the
clouds the players find **Nimbo**, a young cloud-whale calf, with the gathered lantern
light glowing softly inside it. Nimbo took the lanterns because it was lonely in the
empty sky and loved their warmth, not to spoil the festival.

Play it softly. Let the children decide how to reach out: a kind word, an invitation
to the festival, a promise to visit the clouds. There is no fight and no trick. When
they offer friendship, Nimbo lets the light go, the lanterns rise all together, and
Nimbo floats down to join the families, glowing brightest of all. End on that warmth.
The children have won by being kind and full of ideas, which is the only way anyone wins on
the Isles.
