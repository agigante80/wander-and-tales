# The Lost Lantern Festival

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

Tonight is the Festival of Lights. Every year the families of the Floating Isles
send glowing sky lanterns up into the calm evening sky. But when you look out from
the House of the Little Mages, the sky is dark. Lantern Square is grey and quiet.
The lanterns have floated away.

You are little mages. You pick up your bag. You look at each other. "Let us bring
the lights back," you say together. The path to the square is waiting.

*What is your mage name? Which magic do you choose: Light, Grow, Change, or Voice?*

## Stop 1: The Dim Square

You come to Lantern Square. It should be bright and full of song. Instead it is
dark. One last sky lantern sits on a stone, but its little flame has gone out.

A soft grey cat steps out of the mist. It is the Mist Cat. It looks at the cold
lantern, then up at the sky, where a faint trail of light leads toward the clouds.

*How do you light the lantern again? And how could you find out where the others
have gone? Try something and see.*

When the lantern glows once more, it lifts gently into the air and floats up the
trail, as if to show you the way. Up toward the clouds you go.

## Stop 2: The Cloud Bank

At the edge of the island you reach a low bank of soft clouds. Some of the clouds
feel warm, and if you look closely, they glow a little from inside. The lost
lanterns are hiding in there.

A bright Messenger Butterfly flutters down to help.

*Which clouds are hiding lanterns? How can you tell, and how do you reach them up
there? Try something and see.*

When you gather the glowing lanterns out of the clouds, they bob around you in a
happy little cloud of light. Only the biggest lantern of all is still missing.

## Stop 3: The High Ledge

You look up. High above the square, the biggest festival lantern has drifted
against a rocky ledge and caught there. It is too high to reach, and you want to
bring it down gently, with no one in any danger.

The evening breeze curls softly around it.

*How do you bring the big lantern down safely? Could you grow something soft below?
Could you ask the breeze for a gentle nudge? Try something and see.*

When the big lantern floats safely down into your hands, the whole square seems to
hold its breath. There is just one thing left to understand: where did all the
lights go in the first place?

## Nimbo the Cloud-Whale Calf

Up among the clouds you find a small, shy cloud-whale calf. Its name is Nimbo.
Inside its soft cloudy body, a warm light is glowing. It was Nimbo who gathered the
lanterns. Not to spoil the festival. Nimbo was lonely up in the empty sky, and it
loved the warm little lights so much that it wanted to keep them close.

There is no fight here. There never was a real villain.

*What do you say to Nimbo? How can you show it that it is not alone, and that it
can come to the festival too?*

When you offer Nimbo your friendship, it gives a happy little rumble and lets the
gathered light go. All at once the sky lanterns rise together, the whole evening
fills with warm golden light, and Nimbo glows brightest of all as it floats down to
join the festival. The Festival of Lights has begun.

You did it together. You were clever, and you were kind. Well done.
