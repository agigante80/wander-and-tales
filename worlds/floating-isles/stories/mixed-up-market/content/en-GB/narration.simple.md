# The Market of Mixed-Up Magic

*Read aloud, or let an older child read. Stop often to let everyone share their
ideas. There are no wrong answers here, only new ones to try.*

## Before you begin

It is market day on the home island. The Market Row is bright and busy. Every stall
is full: warm bread, ripe fruit, neat shoes, and bright toys.

Then there is a small flash and a soft pop. All at once, everything is in the wrong
place. The baker is holding a pair of boots. The cobbler is holding a kite. The fruit
and the toys have swapped stalls. Nobody can find their things.

You are little mages, and you can put this right. "Let us untangle the market," you
say together.

*What is your mage name? Which magic do you choose: Light, Grow, Change, or Voice?*

## Stop 1: The Muddled Stalls

You walk along the row. Every object sits in the wrong place, looking a little lost.
A pair of boots blinks up at you from the bread stall.

The Mist Cat pads over and sits beside you, ready to help.

*How can you find out where everything belongs? Could you ask the objects
themselves? Try something and see.*

When the objects tell you their homes, you start carrying them back. The easy ones
are soon in the right place again. But a few are still in a muddle.

## Stop 2: The Memory of the Market

To finish, you need to remember how the market looked before the mix-up: who had
what, and where. That is hard, because everything has moved.

A Talking Flower in a pot leans close and hums the market in order, stall by stall,
to help you remember.

*Can you remember which stall had the bread, the fruit, the shoes and the toys?
Match each thing back to the right stall.*

When you set each thing where it began, more of the market is happy again. Only one
tangle is left, and it is a tricky one.

## Stop 3: The Last Swap

Three stalls are still wrong, and they are tangled in a loop. The baker has boots.
The cobbler has a kite. The toymaker has bread. Each one is holding the wrong thing,
and to fix it you must work out who should pass what to whom.

*Who gives their thing to whom, so that everyone ends up with the right one? Talk it
through together.*

When you untangle the last swap, the whole Market Row cheers. Every stall is right
again. But where did the mix-up come from in the first place?

## Pip the New Mage

Behind a quiet stall you find a young mage curled up small. Their name is Pip, and it
is their very first day. Pip tried a Change spell to help, but it went wonky and
muddled the whole market. Pip was so worried, and so shy, that they hid away.

There is no telling-off here. Everyone makes mistakes, especially on a first day.

*What do you say to Pip? How can you show them that it is alright, and that nobody
fixed this alone, you fixed it together?*

When you tell Pip kindly that all is well, they peek out and smile. With one careful
Change, Pip sets the very last thing right, and the whole market claps. Pip stands a
little taller.

You did it together. You were clever, and you were kind. Well done.
