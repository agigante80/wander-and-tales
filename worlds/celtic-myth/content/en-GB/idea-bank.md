# Idea Bank: The Emerald Isles of the Celts

*Improv prompts for the grown-up. When the children want to try something the page
did not plan for, reach in here. Nothing is a wrong answer; every idea bends toward
warmth, imagination, and nobody losing.*

## If the heroes are stuck

- Pook the Puca pops out of a hollow oak as a hare, a hint on his lips, then bounds
  away laughing.
- The Salmon of Wisdom rises in the loch and breathes a single clue in a ring of
  bubbles.
- Old Maeve the Tale-Weaver's voice drifts on the wind with the first line of an old
  tale that holds a hint.
- Let the heroes spend an energy star to "remember a saying their grandmother sang".

## Friendly faces to drop in

- A red deer with dew on its antlers who will share the way for a kind word.
- A wren who has lost her nest of moss and could use a hand.
- An old fisherman mending a net who knows where the mist is thinnest.
- A sleepy badger who only wants someone to share an oatcake by the hedge.

## Gentle troubles to solve

- A path through the oak grove that tangles unless you lay the red wool.
- A loch wrapped so thick in mist that only a song can lift it.
- A row of standing stones to wake in the right order with a gentle touch.
- A little boat that will only carry those who step in calmly, together.

## Ways to win that are not winning

- Make the lonely selkie laugh instead of hurrying past her.
- Trade a honey oatcake for a story rather than for the way through.
- Sing with the swan children until the dusk turns rose and gold.
- Leave the place greener and kinder than you found it.

## Celtic flavour to sprinkle

- The smell of peat smoke and rain on heather, the hush of mist over green hills.
- Celtic knotwork on a bronze brooch, a harp by the hearth, an oak leaf for luck.
- The hum of standing stones at dawn, a salmon leaping, swans gliding on grey water.
