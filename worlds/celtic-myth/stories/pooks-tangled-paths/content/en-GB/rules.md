# Pook's Tangled Paths: Rules for the Grown-Up

The Guide for the Grown-Up explains how the whole game works; read it first if this
is new. This page holds only what is special about this story. A failed roll is never
a defeat; nobody loses. This is a gentle tale: the only "foe" is a lonely faerie who
simply wants a friend, and no one is ever truly hurt.

## Setting up

- Print the map. Each player puts a small figure on the Start, the Roundhouse.
- This world has no magic. Instead each hero leans on one or two of the five
  qualities: **Imagination** (dreaming up ideas), **Courage** (a steady heart),
  **Kindness** (caring for others), **Curiosity** (noticing what others miss), or
  **Patience** (waiting and trying again). On the hero sheet, where it says magic,
  let each child write the quality they like best. Two different qualities work best
  together.
- Give each player five energy stars (five small tokens or bricks). They are spent
  and refilled during play.
- Set out three props if you can: a ball of string for the red wool, a small cake
  or token for the honey oatcake, and a leaf or twig for the sprig of oak. Anything
  can be anything.
- Keep your dice and the dice-band table from the back of the kit beside you.

## The Twist of Fate

Once at each place the players may roll for a twist; read the matching line. The
Guide explains how it works.

| Roll | Twist of Fate |
|---|---|
| 1 | Pook the Puca pops up and gives you one free hint. |
| 2 | You find your courage again, and take back one energy star. |
| 3 | A soft breeze through the oaks, and the next challenge is Easy. |
| 4 | The Salmon of Wisdom blows a lucky bubble. All is well. |
| 5 | A sprig of oak for luck: keep one roll to use again later. |
| 6 | A burst of birdsong, and for a moment everyone feels brave. |

## How it ends

Pook the Puca is the "foe", but he is no villain. He is a playful faerie who made
a game of the paths and then felt sorry for the trouble it caused. The tale is won
not by beating him but by befriending him: sharing the honey oatcake, sitting
beside him, telling him there is a place for him at the fair. Let the kindness
land; it is the whole point. Befriended, Pook gladly untangles every path himself.
See the puzzles page for the three challenges and their solutions.
