# The Journey to the Land of Youth

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

A small friend of the village has gone missing. Yesterday they ran after a white
deer, down to the Misty Loch and across the water, into the Land of Youth. That is
the gentle Otherworld, once called Tir na nOg, where no one ever grows old. It is a
lovely place. That is the danger. The longer your friend stays there, the more they
forget the way home.

Old Maeve the Tale-Weaver calls you to her side. "You two have clever heads and
kind hearts," she says. "Will you cross the mist and bring our friend gently home,
before they forget us altogether?"

She gives you three gifts: a sprig of oak for luck, a honey oatcake to share, and a
ball of red wool. Then she smiles. "Here we do not win by being the strongest. We
win by being clever, brave, and kind."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Cleverness, of Courage, or of Kindness?*

## Stop 1: The Salmon's Riddle

You come down to the shore of the Misty Loch. The water is grey and still. How will
you ever cross?

Then a great silver fish rises up in the ripples. It is the Salmon of Wisdom, old
and bright and kind. "I know the way across," he says, "and I will tell you, if you
can answer my riddle."

*Could you listen closely and think it through together? A brave, thoughtful guess
is allowed too. Try something and see.*

When you answer, the salmon swishes his tail in the water. "Well thought," he says.
He shows you where to wait for the boat.

## Stop 2: The Selkie's Crossing

By the reeds waits Sorcha the Selkie, a gentle seal-person in a soft grey cloak.
She has a little wooden boat. But she is shy, and she looks a little lonely, and she
will not quite meet your eyes.

*This is not a thing to win by force. How could you be kind to her? You could give
her a warm word. You could share your honey oatcake. What do you try?*

When you are kind, Sorcha smiles for the first time in a long while. "Climb in," she
says softly. She rows you out into the mist, and on the far side waits the Land of
Youth.

## Stop 3: The Land of Youth

You step ashore into the Otherworld. The apple trees are all in blossom, the light
is golden, and there is your friend, laughing in the flowers. But when you call
their name, they only half remember you. "Stay," they say. "Stay here forever. It is
always lovely here."

It would be easy to stay. That is the danger. But you remember home: the warm
Roundhouse, the people who love you, the way the loch shines at dawn.

*This is not a fight. How will you help your friend remember home? You could tell
them about home until they smile. You could tie the red wool so you can all find the
way back. What do you do?*

Gently, kindly, you help your friend remember. Their eyes clear. "Home," they say.
"Yes. Let's go home together." You tie the red wool tight, and you all choose,
together, to return.

## Home Across the Mist

Sorcha is waiting in her boat, and she rows you all back across the water, following
the red wool home. The mist parts. There is the green shore, bright in the morning
sun, your friend safe and smiling beside you.

You came home together, and you have a selkie friend now too. High on the green
hills, Danu the Kind smiles down at you. You did it. You were clever, you were
brave, and most of all, you were kind. Well done, heroes.
