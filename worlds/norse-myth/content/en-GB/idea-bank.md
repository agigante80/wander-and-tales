# Idea Bank: The Snowlit Fjords of the North

*Improv prompts for the grown-up. When the children want to try something the page
did not plan for, reach in here. Nothing is a wrong answer; every idea bends toward
warmth, cleverness, and nobody losing.*

## If the heroes are stuck

- Nori the Tomte pops out from under a snowy log, hums a little tune, and points his
  red cap toward the next clue.
- The Two Ravens circle overhead and drop a single feather on the right path.
- Edda the Saga-Keeper's voice drifts on the wind with the first line of an old saga
  that holds a hint.
- Let the heroes spend an energy star to "remember something Grandfather once said".

## Friendly faces to drop in

- A reindeer with frost on its antlers who will share the way for a kind word.
- A pair of otters sliding on the ice who know a shortcut across the fjord.
- A snow hare who has lost a mitten and could use a hand.
- A sleepy bear cub who only wants someone to share the warm by the fire.

## Gentle troubles to solve

- A bridge of ice that will only hold those who cross it gently and together.
- A snowdrift that has buried a friend's doorway, needing many small hands.
- A muddle of footprints in fresh snow to read and follow.
- A lantern gone dark that needs a spark of the everwarm ember and a brave breath.

## Ways to win that are not winning

- Make the frost giant laugh instead of moving him aside.
- Trade a honey bun for a story instead of for the way through.
- Sing with the aurora spirits until they are brave enough to dance.
- Leave the place warmer and kinder than you found it.

## Northern flavour to sprinkle

- The crunch of snow, the smell of woodsmoke, breath misting in the cold.
- Amber windows glowing across the dark, a hearth crackling, wool and fur and warm
  mittens.
- The hush of a forest under snow, and the slow green ripple of the aurora overhead.
