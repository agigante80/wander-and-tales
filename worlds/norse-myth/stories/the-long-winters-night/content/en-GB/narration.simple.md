# The Long Winter's Night

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

Tonight is the longest night of the whole year. In the Hearth Hall, the great fire
that warms the whole valley has gone out. The longhouse is cold. The windows have
stopped glowing amber. Outside, the snow is deep and the dark feels very long.

Edda the Saga-Keeper calls you to her. "You two have clever heads and warm
hearts," she says. "Will you carry the warmth back before dawn?"

She gives you three gifts: the Everwarm Ember, a ball of red yarn, and a warm
honey bun. Then she smiles. "Up here we do not win by being the strongest. We win
by being clever, brave, and kind."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Wit, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: The Frozen Fjord

You step out into the snow and come to the Frozen Fjord. It is a wide sheet of ice
between the mountains, smooth and silver. As you stand on it, the ice groans and
creaks under your feet.

High above, the Two Ravens circle and watch you with bright, friendly eyes.

*How will you cross safely? Could you spread out and step gently, so you are not
all in one heavy heap? Could you hold hands and cross together? You have a ball of
red yarn to lay down as a guide.*

When you cross slowly and kindly, the ice holds firm and lets you over. You reach
the far shore with cold cheeks and warm hearts.

## Stop 2: The Lonely Frost Giant

Past the fjord, a huge giant of pale blue ice sits right in your way. He is taller
than the longhouse, and his face looks fearsome. He folds his big cold arms.

But listen. He shivers. Is he, maybe, a little bit lonely? Nobody ever stops to
talk to him.

*This is not a fight. What kind thing could you do? You have a warm honey bun to
share. You could ask his name. You could offer him a place by the ember. What do
you try?*

When you are kind to him, the giant's icy face melts into a smile. "Thank you for
being my friends," he says softly. And he comes along with you, gentle as snow.

## Stop 3: The Rainbow Bridge

At the foot of the mountain shimmers the Rainbow Bridge, made of soft colours, rising
up to the high halls. It only holds firm for those who cross with a kind and honest
heart.

*Do you cross with a good heart? You carry the warm Everwarm Ember, and now you
have a new friend beside you. Step out together, gently and true.*

The bridge glows bright under your feet and holds you all the way up. At the high
hall you hold out the Everwarm Ember, and the world's warmth catches light again.

## Dawn and Warmth

Far below, one by one, the hearth-fires relight across the whole valley. Every
window glows warm amber again. The long, dark night turns soft and golden, and the
first light of dawn slips over the white mountains.

Up in the sky, the Aurora Spirits dance in teal and green and purple. The Lonely
Frost Giant has friends at last, and he is smiling. And Freya the Kind looks down
and smiles too. You did it together. You were clever, you were brave, and most of
all, you were kind. Well done, heroes.
