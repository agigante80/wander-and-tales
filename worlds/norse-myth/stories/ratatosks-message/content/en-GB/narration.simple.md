# Ratatosk's Muddled Message

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

The Great World Tree is huge. It goes from deep roots far below to a high nest
right at the top. All day, a quick red squirrel runs up and down it, carrying
everyone's messages. His name is Ratatosk.

But today something has gone wrong. Ratatosk has muddled all the messages up! Now
the great Eagle at the very top thinks a small friend down in the roots said
something unkind. And the small friend thinks the Eagle said something unkind too.
Neither of them did. It was only a mix-up. But now the two old friends are cross,
and the whole tree feels sad.

Edda the Saga-Keeper calls you to the warm Hearth Hall. "You have bright ideas and
kind hearts," she says. "Will you help Ratatosk sort his messages and set things
right, so the two friends are friends again?"

She gives you three gifts: a ball of red yarn, a snack for the squirrel, and a
little lantern. Then she smiles. "Up here we do not win by being the strongest. We
win by being brave, kind, and full of ideas."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: Catch the Squirrel

You step out under the Great World Tree. There is Ratatosk, zooming up and down,
round and round, far too flustered to stop. "Sorry, sorry, no time, all muddled!"
he chatters as he whizzes past.

He is in such a flap that he cannot help anyone, not even himself. He needs to
slow down and rest.

*How could you help him feel calm? You have a snack for the squirrel. Could you
sit very still and speak softly? Could you watch which way he runs and wait for
the right moment?*

When you hold out the snack and speak gently, Ratatosk stops at last. He nibbles,
he breathes, and his little heart slows down. "Thank you," he sighs. "Now I can
think."

## Stop 2: Sort the Messages

Ratatosk opens his paws, and out tumble three little messages, all in a jumble.
There are three friends waiting: the Eagle at the top, a kind old owl on a middle
branch, and the small friend down in the roots. Each message belongs to one of
them, but which goes where?

The Two Ravens, Thought and Memory, land beside you. They like to give a hint to
heroes who try hard.

*Can you remember who lives where? Read each message and look for a clue. One talks
about the wind up high. One talks about the deep dark roots. One hoots a friendly
hello. Which friend does each one belong to?*

When you match every message to the right friend, the muddle melts away. "That's
it! That's it!" cheers Ratatosk, clapping his little paws. Now you know exactly
what to carry, and to whom.

## Stop 3: Climb and Deliver

There is one more thing to do. The right message must go all the way up to the
Eagle, and the right message all the way down to the small friend, so they each
understand at last.

The tree is tall, and the climb is high. But you have your ball of red yarn.

*How will you climb safely and not get lost? Could you tie the red yarn at the
bottom, like a safety line, and follow it as you go? Could you light your little
lantern for the dark deep roots?*

When you tie on the red yarn and climb carefully, you reach the Eagle high above.
You read out the kind, true message. The Eagle's fierce frown softens. "Oh!" he
says. "So my friend was never unkind at all." Then you follow the yarn down, down,
down to the roots, lantern glowing, and tell the small friend the same. The small
friend's eyes go wide and happy.

## Friends Again

The Eagle spreads his great wings and waves from the top of the tree. Far below,
the small friend waves right back, and they both call out, "Friends again!"

Ratatosk runs a happy loop around the whole tree. "You sorted my muddle and mended
my friends," he says. "Thank you, thank you!"

High above the snowy fjords, Freya the Kind looks down and smiles. You did it
together. You were full of ideas, you were brave, and most of all, you were kind. Well
done, heroes.
