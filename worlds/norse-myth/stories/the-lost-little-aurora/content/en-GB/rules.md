# The Lost Little Aurora: Rules for the Grown-Up

*New to this? Read the Guide for the Grown-Up first. It explains the whole idea in
two pages and you will never need it again.*

The Guide for the Grown-Up explains how the game works. This page holds only what is
special about this story. A failed roll is never a defeat; nobody loses. This is a
gentle tale, soft and warm, where good ideas and kindness win every time and no one
is ever truly hurt.

## Setting up

- Print the map. Each player puts a small figure on the Start, the Hearth Hall.
- This world has no magic. Instead each hero leans on one or two of the five qualities:
  **Imagination** (dreaming up ideas), **Courage** (a steady heart), **Heart** (caring
  for others), **Patience** (waiting and trying again), or **Kindness** (a warm helping
  hand). On the hero sheet, where it says magic, let each child write the
  quality they like best. Two different qualities work best together.
- Give each player five energy stars (five small tokens or bricks). They are spent
  and refilled during play.
- Set out three props if you can: a small light or token for the little lantern, a
  ball of red string for the red yarn, and any small thing at all for the honey bun.
  Anything can be anything.
- Keep your dice and the dice-band table from the back of the kit beside you.

## The Twist of Fate

Once at each place the players may roll for a twist; read the matching line. The
Guide explains how it works.

| Roll | Twist of Fate |
|---|---|
| 1 | Nori the Tomte pops up and gives you one free hint. |
| 2 | You find your courage again, and take back one energy star. |
| 3 | A friendly wind off the fjord, and the next challenge is Easy. |
| 4 | The Two Ravens fly over and nod kindly. All is well. |
| 5 | A lucky strand of red yarn: keep one roll to use again later. |
| 6 | A burst of warmth from the hearth, and for a moment everyone feels brave. |

## How it ends

The little aurora is too shy and too cold to glow, but she is no villain and there
is no foe in this tale at all. It is won not by beating anyone but by finding her,
helping her gather her three colours, and lending her the courage to leap back into
the sky. Let the kindness land; it is the whole point. See the puzzles page for the
three challenges and their solutions.
