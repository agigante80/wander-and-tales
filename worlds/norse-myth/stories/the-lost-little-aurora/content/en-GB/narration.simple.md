# The Lost Little Aurora

*Read aloud, slowly and warmly. Stop often to let the children answer. There are
no wrong answers here, only brave new ideas to try.*

## Before you begin

It is winter in the fjords, and the long night should be full of colour. On clear
nights the Aurora Spirits dance, and the sky glows teal and green and purple. But
tonight the sky stays dark. Something is wrong.

The littlest Aurora Spirit has tumbled down from the sky. She fell into the snowy
Whispering Pines, and she lost her colours. Now she is too shy and too cold to
glow.

Edda the Saga-Keeper calls you to the warm Hearth Hall. "You two have bright ideas
and kind hearts," she says. "Will you find the little aurora and help her shine
again?"

She gives you three gifts: a little lantern, a ball of red yarn, and a warm honey
bun. Then she smiles. "Up here we do not win by being the strongest. We win by
being brave, kind, and full of ideas."

*What is your hero name? Are you a hero of Imagination, of Courage, or of Heart?*

## Stop 1: The Dark Forest

You step out into the Whispering Pines. The snow is deep and soft. The tall trees
stand dark and quiet all around you.

Somewhere in here, the little aurora is hiding. She is shy and cold and very, very
small. You will have to look closely to find her.

*How will you find her? Could you move softly and look for a tiny glimmer of light?
Could you follow little footprints in the snow? Could you ask Nori the Tomte, the
hearth sprite in the red cap, to help?*

When you look gently, you spot a soft glimmer behind a tree. There she is, the
littlest aurora, no bigger than your two hands, dim and shivering. "Hello," you
whisper. "We came to help you."

## Stop 2: The Three Colours

The little aurora is happy you found her. But she cannot glow. "I lost my three
colours when I fell," she says sadly. "Teal, and green, and purple. They are
hiding in the world now."

Look around. The colours did not go far. They are all near you.

*Can you find her three colours? Where is teal? Where is green? Where is purple?
Look at the icicles, look at the pines, look up at the sky.*

You find teal in a shining icicle. You find green in a dark pine branch. You find
purple high up in the twilight sky. One by one, the little aurora gathers her
colours back. She begins to glow, soft and warm.

## Stop 3: The Climb to the Sky

The little aurora glows now, but she is still afraid. "The sky is so high," she
says. "What if I cannot reach it? What if I fall again?"

She needs your courage to lend her some of her own.

*How will you help her be brave? Could you climb a tall pine with her? Could you
cheer her on? You have a ball of red yarn. Could you give her a gentle boost up?*

You climb the tallest pine. You tie the red yarn so she can hold on. You count
together, one, two, three. "You can do it!" you call. The little aurora takes a
deep breath, lets go, and leaps up into the dark.

## The Sky Dances Again

Up she flies, higher and higher, glowing bright. The other Aurora Spirits swoop
down to meet her, laughing. "You came back! You came back!" they sing.

Then the whole sky bursts into colour. Teal, and green, and purple, all dancing
together over the snowy fjord. The long night is dark no more.

High among the stars, Freya the Kind looks down and smiles at you. You did it
together. You were full of ideas, you were brave, and most of all, you were kind. Well
done, heroes of the fjords.
