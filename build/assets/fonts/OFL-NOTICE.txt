Vendored fonts and their licences
=================================

All typefaces bundled here are free/libre and redistributable.

DejaVu Sans, DejaVu Serif
  Licence: DejaVu Fonts Licence (a permissive, free licence; Bitstream Vera
  derivative). Used for the kit prose and as the graceful fallback faces.

Quicksand (Quicksand-SemiBold.ttf), Nunito (Nunito-Regular.ttf,
Nunito-Bold.ttf), Caveat (Caveat-SemiBold.ttf)
  Licence: SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
  Sources: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com). Each is a single static
  weight instanced from the upstream variable font with fontTools, used only
  for the adventure sheet: Quicksand for display headings, Nunito for body
  text, and Caveat as the handwriting face for the pre-filled example answers.

Designers: Quicksand by Andrew Paglinawan; Nunito by Vernon Adams, Cyreal and
Jacques Le Bailly; Caveat by Impallari Type (Pablo Impallari).

The SIL Open Font Licence permits redistribution and embedding in documents
(including the generated PDFs); the fonts are not sold on their own.
